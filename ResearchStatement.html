Research Statement

My research interests center on astrocytes and their role in neurological disorders, particularly how these cells can be leveraged to improve treatments for neurodegenerative diseases. During the Summer Ventures in Science and Mathematics program at UNC Wilmington, I worked in the Wang Lab, where we investigated the ability of lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) carrying GFP-encoding mRNA to deliver genetic material into astrocytes. Building on this experience, I hope to investigate how differences in LNP composition and delivery efficiency affect the uptake and expression of therapeutic genetic material in astrocytes. Understanding these relationships could help advance astrocyte-targeted gene delivery strategies and contribute to the development of treatments for neurodegenerative disorders.
